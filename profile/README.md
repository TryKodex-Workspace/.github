## About Us

**TryKodex** is an independent software studio co-founded by **Mehmet Sinan Eldemir** and **Mert**.

We turn ideas into digital products—from the first concept and interface to development, testing, and continuous improvement. Our work spans mobile and web applications, AI-powered tools, and games, with a shared focus on usability, clarity, and meaningful experiences.

We're a small team with a hands-on approach: we experiment, build, learn, and refine.

## What We Build

- **Applications & platforms** — Practical products designed around real needs.
- **Games & interactive experiences** — Social, accessible, and engaging ways to play.
- **AI-powered tools** — Thoughtful applications of AI to simplify creative and everyday workflows.
- **Productivity solutions** — Tools that make organizing and collaborating easier.

## Our Approach

**Build with purpose. Design with care. Improve through iteration.**

We value intuitive user experiences, maintainable software, and focused execution. Rather than adding complexity for its own sake, we aim to make products that feel simple, reliable, and enjoyable to use.
